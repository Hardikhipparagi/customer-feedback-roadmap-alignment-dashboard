# Customer Feedback & Product Roadmap Alignment Dashboard

## Project Overview
A non-coding Product Management analytics project that converts customer feedback into structured insights, prioritizes product opportunities, and aligns customer demand with a product roadmap.

## Business Problem
Product teams receive feedback from surveys, support, reviews, sales, customer success and usage data. The challenge is separating recurring customer problems from isolated requests and deciding what deserves roadmap investment.

## Objective
Create an evidence-based flow:
**Customer Voice → Pain Point → Customer Impact → Business Value → Strategic Alignment → Priority → Product Decision → Roadmap**

## Dataset
- 120 realistic customer feedback records
- 15 roadmap features
- Customer segments, industries, subscription plans, sources, sentiment, CSAT, NPS, severity, revenue impact, retention risk and prioritization metrics.

## Prioritization Methodology
Requests are evaluated using frequency, customer segment importance, pain severity, customer impact, revenue impact, retention risk, strategic alignment and development effort.

## Impact vs Effort
- High impact + low effort: Quick Wins
- High impact + high effort: Strategic Initiatives
- Low impact + low effort: Fill-ins
- Low impact + high effort: Avoid/Postpone

## Scenario Analysis
Three stakeholder weighting scenarios are included:
1. Customer-First: 60% feedback / 20% business / 20% sentiment
2. Business-First: 30% feedback / 50% business / 20% sentiment
3. Balanced: 40% feedback / 40% business / 20% sentiment

## Dashboard
The Excel dashboard includes executive KPIs, top requested features, sentiment mix, prioritization analysis and impact/effort guidance.

## Key KPI Snapshot
- Total feedback: 120
- Roadmap alignment: 100.0%
- Average CSAT: 3.27/5
- NPS: -27.5
- Positive sentiment: 43.3%
- Negative sentiment: 19.2%
- High-severity issues: 47
- High-priority requests: 98

## Product Recommendations
1. Address high-impact, high-priority requests first.
2. Use quick wins to improve customer experience without major engineering investment.
3. Review high-value enterprise/security requests even when request volume is lower.
4. Keep strategically unaligned requests in Monitor/Reject rather than automatically adding them to the roadmap.
5. Revisit priorities as customer evidence, usage data and business strategy change.
